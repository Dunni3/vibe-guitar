4 · For capturing your guitar's body sound

The under-saddle piezo hears the strings but not the wooden body, which is why plugged-in acoustics sound thin. To correct that, record the piezo and a good microphone at the same moment, then fit a filter on your laptop that turns the piezo track into the mic track. The Pi then runs that filter live. You only need this gear for the measurement sessions, not for playing.

Designing new sounds: nothing extra to buy

It's all software on the hardware above.

  1. Write or generate an effect on your laptop or on the Pi over SSH, in C++ or Faust, by hand or with an AI coding tool.
  2. Build and benchmark it on the Pi. The profiling harness renders test recordings through it and reports the smallest buffer it can run at safely.
  3. Save it as a preset with its settings and buffer size, so the web app can list it.
  4. Switch to it from your phone between songs. Loading can take a second; that's fine, because nothing changes while you're playing.

Putting it together

  1. Flash the SD card on your laptop with Raspberry Pi Imager. Set the Wi-Fi network, a username and SSH in the Imager's settings so the Pi never needs a screen or keyboard.
  2. Stick on the Active Cooler. Peel the thermal pads, line up the two push-pins with the holes by the chip, press until they click, and plug its little cable into the fan socket.
  3. Mount the Pi on the case base using the standoffs that come with the Pisound case.
  4. Press the Pisound onto the 40-pin header and screw it to the tall standoffs. It doesn't have to sit flush against the plastic of the header; the standoffs set the height.
  5. Close the case, insert the SD card, plug in the 27 W supply, and SSH in from your laptop.
  6. First sound: guitar (via preamp, if you have one) into the Pisound input, Pisound output into the amp, and run a simple pass-through to check levels with the Pisound's gain knob.

Don't buy these

A USB audio interface for the live boxThe Pisound replaces it and avoids the extra USB buffering. The interface in section 4 is only for recording measurements on your laptop.
HiFiBerry DAC+ ADCIts input is built for mics and line level; guitar sounds muffled through it.
Soldering iron or GPIO header kitsThe Pisound comes assembled, and the case includes the standoffs.
A Pisound older than v1.2Earlier versions have taller jacks that hit the Pi 5's cooler. New stock is v1.2; double-check if buying used.
Monitor, keyboard, micro-HDMI cableYou'll run it headless and talk to it over Wi-Fi.
A big neural amp model setupNot needed for acoustic tones, and the one workload that could strain a Pi core.
